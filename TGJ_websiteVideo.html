Remarks (My Appearance)

  • Jerseys: Red, Green, or White (turned inside out).
  • Numbers: 15 or 9.
  • Hair & Gear: Tied up or center part, wearing a pink headguard.

This highlight video tells the whole story of my rugby career.

It includes footage from matches ranging from as far back as 2023 to as recent as last month.

You might be surprised to see clips of several missed kicks in the highlight video. Those mistakes fueled my next kick, my next play. If I hadn't made those mistakes, I wouldn't have practiced kicking, or rugby, as much afterward. Even those few missed kicks are irreplaceable events in my rugby story.

And from an outsider's perspective, there are several plays that might not seem flashy enough to be included in the highlights. However, like the missed kicks, those are all irreplaceable events in my rugby career, each one representing my own progress and development.
