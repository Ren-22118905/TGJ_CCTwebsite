Welcome to My Story

Where Passion Meets Heritage

The concept of "Life Echoes" represents the idea that every path I have walked resonates deeply within me, harmonizing to shape who I am today. It is the powerful echo of heavy footsteps on the rugby field and the memory of hard-fought matches, seamlessly blending with the quiet, still moments captured through my lens amidst the historic streets of Kyoto and Osaka.

While athletics and photography may seem like two entirely different worlds, they are intimately interconnected within my journey. The resilience and focus forged on the rugby pitch sharpen my vision to perceive the subtle beauty of the world, while the timeless dignity of ancient Japanese architecture inspires a quiet passion that fuels my next play. Past challenges and sacred traditions cross paths, echoing together to sound out a new melody for the future.
